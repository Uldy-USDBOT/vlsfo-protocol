1. What is VLSFO? Very Low Sulphur Fuel Oil Explained

VLSFO (Very Low Sulphur Fuel Oil) is a marine bunker fuel with a maximum sulphur content of 0.50% m/m, mandated by the IMO 2020 regulation. It replaced High Sulphur Fuel Oil (HSFO) as the primary marine fuel for vessels operating in international waters.

3. VLSFO vs HSFO: IMO 2020 Compliance and Sulphur Content

Before IMO 2020, vessels predominantly burned High Sulphur Fuel Oil at 3.5% sulphur. The regulation mandated a switch to 0.50% sulphur-compliant fuel. The key differences between these fuels affect engine efficiency and environmental compliance.

Many owners also evaluate Low Sulphur Marine Gas Oil (LSMGO) as an alternative. VLSFO is typically cheaper than MGO but requires heating and more careful compatibility management.

4. VLSFO Technical Specs: Density, Viscosity and ISO 8217

VLSFO must meet ISO 8217 specifications. Key parameters include density (typically 920–970 kg/m³ at 15°C), viscosity (20–180 cSt at 50°C), and CCAI ratings that affect fuel behavior and combustion characteristics.

Poor blending stability can cause sludge formation and filter blockages. Engineers are advised to verify compatibility, cat fines content, and pour point before bunkering.
