VLSFO Trading & Bunker Fuel Market

What is VLSFO?

VLSFO (Very Low Sulphur Fuel Oil) is a marine bunker fuel containing a maximum sulphur content of 0.50% m/m (mass/mass). Mandated by the International Maritime Organization (IMO) 2020 regulation, VLSFO replaced High Sulphur Fuel Oil and has become the industry standard for compliance with environmental regulations.

Gibraltar's Role in Global VLSFO Markets

  • Primary Supply Hub: Distributes millions of tons of VLSFO annually to vessels worldwide
  • Price Benchmark: Gibraltar prices set global reference rates and influence worldwide fuel markets
  • Quality Assurance: Renowned for supplying premium-grade VLSFO meeting strict ISO 8217 standards
  • Advanced Logistics: Modern bunkering infrastructure enables rapid, secure fuel delivery
  • Competitive Advantage: Strategic location reduces delivery times and transportation costs
📊 Market Scale: Approximately 5,000+ vessels are supplied with bunker fuel monthly in the Gibraltar region, totaling over 10 million metric tons of VLSFO annually.
